THE VARADARAJA STORY

From Theatre to Multiplex

SINCE 1979

A Cinema Legacy in Chennai

Public corporate records show Varadaraja Theatres Private Limited was incorporated on 25 May 1979. The cinema has evolved with its audience into the modern Varadaraja Cinemas multiplex.

THE NEW VARADHARAJA

Four Screens. One Destination.

The current public profile highlights four screens, 4K/RGB laser projection, Dolby Atmos, Harkness silver screens, luxurious seating and rotary parking.
