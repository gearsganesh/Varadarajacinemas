The Varadharaja Story

From Theatre to Multiplex

A Chennai cinema story carried forward into a new four-screen experience.
Since 1979

A Cinema Legacy in Chennai

Varadharaja's cinema journey began in 1979, building a neighbourhood identity around the simple pleasure of coming together for a film. That legacy continues today with a renewed cinema experience in Chitlapakkam, connecting the name people know with a more modern way to watch, listen and enjoy the big screen.

The New Varadharaja

Four Screens. One Destination.

The new Varadharaja brings four screens together under one destination, designed around sharper 4K RGB laser presentation, immersive Dolby Atmos sound and comfortable seating. Whether it is a family outing, a weekend blockbuster or a late-night show, the idea remains the same: make the cinema itself part of the experience.
